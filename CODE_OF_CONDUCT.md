# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Panvas project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them; and
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission;
- conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting; or
- other behavior that would be considered unwelcome in this project community.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate it, and may take other actions they deem appropriate to keep the project welcoming.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct may be removed from the project team by the project owner.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces. It may also apply when behavior outside project spaces materially affects another participant's safety or ability to participate.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers at [sksumitahmed007@gmail.com](mailto:sksumitahmed007@gmail.com). You may also contact the maintainer through the repository's private GitHub mechanisms. Do not include passwords, tokens, private notes, or other sensitive data in a report.

Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of the reporter when handling an incident. Please do not use public issues or discussions for sensitive conduct details.
